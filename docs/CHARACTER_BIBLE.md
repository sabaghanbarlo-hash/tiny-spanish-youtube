# Tiny Spanish: Character Bible

Four original characters. None is a copy of the Tiny Japanese or Tiny French cast (Mika/Alex, Adrien/Marinette, Alia, Nino, Chloé, Lucas). Each has a different silhouette, hair shape and dominant color so they are readable at phone size.

## Emma Brooks, The Beginner
- Personality: curious, enthusiastic, nervous about mistakes, proud of small wins.
- Look: fair skin, light freckles, sandy-blonde ponytail with side-swept bangs, green eyes.
- Outfit: mint hoodie, light jeans, white sneakers, backpack with a travel patch.
- Role in lessons: asks the beginner question, makes the classic mistake, repeats the phrase out loud.
- Image prompt: "Flat 2D cartoon character, young woman, fair skin with freckles, sandy-blonde ponytail and side-swept bangs, green eyes, mint-green hoodie, light jeans, white sneakers, small backpack with a travel patch, friendly smile, clean outlines, front view, neutral pose, plain background, consistent character sheet"

## Mateo Ruiz, The Native Speaker
- Personality: patient, funny, warm; corrects with a joke, never shames.
- Look: warm brown skin, short dark wavy hair, light stubble, dark-brown eyes.
- Outfit: terracotta shirt with rolled sleeves over a cream tee, dark chinos, brown boots.
- Role: gives the natural way to say it, explains why locals say it.
- Image prompt: "Flat 2D cartoon character, man in his late twenties, warm brown skin, short dark wavy hair, light stubble, kind dark-brown eyes, terracotta button shirt with rolled sleeves over cream tee, dark chinos, brown boots, relaxed grin, clean outlines, front view, plain background, character sheet"

## Camila "Cami" Torres, The Funny Friend
- Personality: energetic, dramatic, lovable chaos; funny misunderstandings, always hungry.
- Look: tan skin, long dark hair in two high space buns with loose curls, red lipstick, gold hoops.
- Outfit: magenta bomber with a sunflower pin, yellow top, black high-waist pants, yellow sneakers.
- Role: creates the funny situation, over-reacts, says the wrong word on purpose.
- Image prompt: "Flat 2D cartoon character, young woman, tan skin, long dark hair in two high space buns with loose curls, red lipstick, large gold hoop earrings, expressive eyebrows, magenta bomber jacket with sunflower pin, yellow top, black pants, yellow sneakers, big laugh, clean outlines, front view, plain background, character sheet"

## Santiago "Santi" Herrera, The Travel Guide
- Personality: calm, curious, always filming; dry humor; loves local expressions.
- Look: medium-brown skin, tall and lean, curly dark hair, round sunglasses on his head, short beard.
- Outfit: teal utility vest, mustard tee, khaki shorts, hiking sandals, small camera on a strap.
- Role: opens travel scenes (airport, hotel, directions), adds a culture note.
- Image prompt: "Flat 2D cartoon character, tall lean man around thirty, medium-brown skin, curly dark hair, round sunglasses pushed up on head, short beard, teal utility vest over mustard tee, khaki shorts, hiking sandals, small camera on strap, calm smile, clean outlines, front view, plain background, character sheet"

## Consistency rules
1. Hair, outfit colors and proportions never change between episodes (seasonal props like umbrellas and scarves are allowed).
2. Name tag color matches the palette in `config/characters.json`.
3. Two to three characters in a Reel, never more than four on screen.
4. The existing Tiny engines draw characters in code (Pillow rigs), not with image generation. The rigs for this cast are built in Stage 5 from the specs above, and the reference sheet is rendered from the rig, so every scene uses the same drawing.
